## The limits of AI prediction, and how to use it correctly

### What AI can't do

No matter how accurate it is, AI has real limits.

- **It can't detect last-minute condition changes or trouble** — a racer's condition on the day isn't fully reflected in the data
- **Start timing isn't perfectly predictable** — flying starts and late starts are inherently hard to forecast
- **It can't fully predict odds movement** — a large last-minute bet can shift expected value right before the window closes
- **A 100% hit rate is impossible** — no matter how good the AI is, some races will always be missed

### How to use AI predictions correctly

1. **Treat it as one input among several** — don't take the AI's pick at face value; check the race information yourself too
2. **Be selective about races** — instead of betting on everything, focus on the races the AI is most confident about
3. **Manage your bankroll strictly** — set a daily budget and don't chase a single big win
4. **Take a long-term view** — judge performance over a month, not by a single day's win or loss
5. **Use the Upset Index as a guide** — bet conservatively on "Favorite friendly" races, and watch for big payouts on "High upset chance" ones
