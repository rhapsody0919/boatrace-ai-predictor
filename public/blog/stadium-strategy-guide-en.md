## What actually differs between venues?

Here's what varies from venue to venue.

### 1. Water type (fresh, salt, or brackish)

- **Fresh water**: lower buoyancy, favors lighter racers
- **Salt water**: higher buoyancy, weight differences matter less
- **Brackish water**: a mix of fresh and salt, conditions shift with the tide

### 2. Water surface size

- **A wide water surface**: overtakes and inside passes are easier, favors outside lanes
- **A narrow water surface**: favors the inside lane, wire-to-wire wins land more often

### 3. Wind effects

- **A windy venue**: harder starts, more prone to volatility
- **A calm venue**: standard theory holds, favors lane 1

### 4. Tides (salt-water and brackish venues only)

- **High tide**: higher water level, easier to build speed
- **Low tide**: lower water level, turning ability matters more

These factors combine to shape each venue's individual character.

## Regional tendencies

### Kanto region (Toda, Edogawa, Heiwajima, Kiryu, Tamagawa)

- Generally prone to volatility
- Wind has a significant effect
- Standard theory often doesn't hold

**Strategy:** Weight the exhibition run heavily, stay mindful of upset potential

---

### Chubu-Tokai region (Hamanako, Gamagori, Tokoname, Tsu)

- Mostly balanced venues
- Affected by both wind and tide
- Motor performance matters

**Strategy:** Check the motor 2nd-place-or-better rate, and weigh both sides for a balanced bet

---

### Kinki region (Biwako, Suminoe, Amagasaki)

- Relatively predictable
- Mostly fresh water
- Racer ability tends to translate directly into results

**Strategy:** Weight racer win rate heavily and lean toward the favorite

---

### Chugoku-Shikoku region (Miyajima, Tokuyama, Shimonoseki, Marugame, Kojima, Naruto)

- Many inside-favoring venues
- Calm water facing the Seto Inland Sea
- Lane 1 is highly reliable

**Strategy:** Bet around a lane-1 anchor with a favorite-focused approach

---

### Kyushu region (Wakamatsu, Ashiya, Fukuoka, Karatsu, Omura)

- The region with the strongest inside advantage
- Mostly calm-wind venues
- Lane 1's wire-to-wire rate runs high

**Strategy:** A lane-1-anchored favorite-focused bet; Trio and Quinella bets are efficient here
