## Common failure patterns and how to avoid them

### Failure pattern 1: Getting overconfident and raising your bet size

**The bad pattern:**
```
Trifecta hits 2 times in a row
→ "I'm on a roll today!"
→ Bets ¥1,000 on the next race
→ Misses and loses all the profit
```

**How to avoid it:**
No matter how well things are going, **never change your bet size**.

### Failure pattern 2: Trying to win back a loss

**The bad pattern:**
```
5 races in a row miss
→ "I'll win it back on the next one!"
→ Doubles the bet size
→ Misses again, and the loss grows bigger
```

**How to avoid it:**
On a losing day, **stop right there**. Start again the next day at the same bet size.

### Failure pattern 3: Chasing only big payouts

**The bad pattern:**
```
Chasing a massive Trifecta payout every time
→ With a 5.6% hit rate, it only hits about once every 18 tries
→ Your bankroll runs dry during the other 17
```

**How to avoid it:**
Stick to a balance of **70% Place, 20% Trio, 10% Trifecta**.
