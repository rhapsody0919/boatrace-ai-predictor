# The Beginner's Guide to Getting Started with Boat Racing [From Buying a Ticket to Using AI Predictions]

## Introduction

"I want to try boat racing, but where do I even start?"

This article walks **complete beginners** through starting boat racing from scratch. Basic rules, bet types, how to register for TELEBOAT (Japan's official online betting platform), and how to use AI predictions — this one article gives you the full picture.

## The basic rules

Let's start with the minimum rules you need to know.

- **6 boats** race around a 600m water course for **3 laps (1,800m total)** to determine the finishing order
- A race is decided in about 1 minute 50 seconds — the fastest-paced of Japan's public-authorized betting sports
- Held daily, morning to night, at 24 boat racing venues across Japan
- Each boat has a lane number from 1 (white) to 6 (green), and **lane 1 (the inside lane) has the biggest advantage** (a national average win rate of ~55%)

✅ **Beginner tip:** If lane 1 has a strong racer, that race is easier to predict. Start by "anchoring around lane 1."

## The bet types

Boat racing offers 7 bet types. As a beginner, learn them from the top of this list down.

| Bet type | What it means | Hit probability | Typical payout |
|-----------|------|---------|---------|
| **Win** | Pick the 1st-place finisher | 1/6 (~17%) | ¥100–500 |
| **Place** | Pick a top-2 finisher | 2/6 (~33%) | ¥100–200 |
| **Quinella** | Pick the top 2, any order | 1/15 (~7%) | ¥500–2,000 |
| **Exacta** | Pick the top 2 in exact order | 1/30 (~3%) | ¥1,000–5,000 |
| **Trio** | Pick the top 3, any order | 1/20 (~5%) | ¥500–3,000 |
| **Trifecta** | Pick the top 3 in exact order | 1/120 (~0.8%) | ¥3,000–tens of thousands |
| **Quinella Place** | Pick 2 boats that both finish top 2 | 3/15 (~20%) | ¥100–500 |

⭐ **Quinella is the recommended bet for beginners.** It has a good balance of hit probability and payout, and anchoring around lane 1 gives even a beginner a real shot.

## How to register for TELEBOAT (buying tickets online)

**TELEBOAT** is the official service that lets you buy tickets from your phone or PC, without visiting a venue in person.

### Registration steps

1. Go to the **official TELEBOAT site** (teleboat.jp)
2. Click "New Member Registration"
3. Enter your name, address, date of birth, and other personal details
4. Register a **designated Japanese bank account** (for deposits and withdrawals)
5. Upload identity verification documents
6. After the review is complete, you'll receive a login ID and password
7. Deposit funds, and you can buy tickets right away

Note: TELEBOAT is a domestic Japanese service — registration requires a Japanese bank account and ID, so it's realistically available to residents of Japan.

### Major supported banks

- Japan Post Bank
- MUFG Bank
- Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation
- Mizuho Bank
- Rakuten Bank
- PayPay Bank, and others

⚠️ **Note:** Registration can take a few days. Deciding "I want to bet!" right when you're watching a race won't leave you time, so it's best to register in advance.

## Beginner-friendly ways to bet

### Step 1: Start with Quinella

- **Anchor around lane 1**, and pick 2–3 opponents
- Keep it to 2–3 combinations
- Example: bet 1=2, 1=3, 1=4 (3 combinations)

### Step 2: Move to Trio once you're comfortable

- Anchor around lane 1, and pick 3–4 candidates for 2nd/3rd
- Keep it to 3–6 combinations
- Payouts get bigger, so you can target a return even with a small stake

### Step 3: Try Exacta and Trifecta once you're confident

- Predicting exact order raises the difficulty, but also the payout
- Using AI predictions as a reference improves your accuracy

## How to think about your starting budget

The biggest mistake beginners make is **suddenly betting a large sum of money**. Stick to these rules:

- Keep your **per-race spend to ¥500–1,000**
- Set a daily cap of **¥3,000–5,000**
- Don't bet on every race — **only bet on races you feel confident about**
- Don't try to chase back a loss

✅ **A guideline:** Aim to spend **under ¥10,000 a month** during your first month. At this stage, **developing an eye for reading races** matters more than winning.

## Mistakes beginners should avoid

⚠️ These are the common failure patterns beginners fall into.

- **Betting on every race** → have the courage to pass on races you're not confident about
- **Chasing your losses** → getting emotional clouds your judgment and leads to further losses
- **Only betting Trifecta** → the hit rate is too low, and your bankroll disappears fast
- **Betting without checking the odds** → even a hit can end up losing you money (a break-even bet)
- **Not looking at the data** → predicting purely on gut feeling loses in the long run
- **Not setting a budget** → overspending starts affecting your daily life

✅ Boat racing rewards **calm, data-driven judgment**. Use AI predictions and bet based on data, not emotion.
