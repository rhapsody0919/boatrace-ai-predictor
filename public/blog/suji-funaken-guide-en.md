# What Is a "Suji" Bet? A Quick-Reference Guide to Venue-Specific Patterns [Omura Lane 1 Win Rate ~60%, etc.]

## Introduction

Looking at a bet like "1-2-3" or "4-5-6," could you explain **why that particular combination**?

Boat racing has a concept called "suji" — **a theory of how a race tends to unfold**. Understanding suji lets you build your bets by **imagining how the race will actually play out**, instead of just picking numbers.

This article covers everything from the basics of suji betting to how to apply it in practice.

---

## What is a suji bet?

### Definition

A **suji bet** is a bet built around boat racing's theory of race development.

In boat racing, the battle for the first turn mark on lap 1 (the "1 mark") essentially decides the race. And there's a general rule: **which boat takes the lead at that turn strongly influences the pattern of finishers behind it.**

### Why does suji exist?

Boat racing courses turn counterclockwise. Going around the first mark:

- **Inside boats**: have a shorter turning arc and can turn first
- **Outside boats**: have a longer turning arc and tend to fall behind

Because of this, **the winning boat's lane determines which boats are likely to finish 2nd and 3rd.**

## Basic suji patterns

### Inside wire-to-wire suji (when lane 1 wins)

**The most basic pattern — lane 1 nails the start and wins wire-to-wire.**

| Development | Suji combination | Reason |
|------|--------|------|
| Lane 1 wire-to-wire | 1-2, 1-3 | The inside lanes 2 and 3 follow naturally |
| Lane 1 wire-to-wire (inside pass) | 1-4, 1-2-4 | Lane 4 slips inside for 2nd |

**Typical bets:**
```
1-2-3 (the classic favorite combination)
1-2-4 (an inside-pass pattern)
1-3-2 (lane 3 ahead of lane 2)
1-3-4
```

**Key points:**
- When lane 1 wins wire-to-wire, the outside lanes 5 and 6 generally don't factor in
- "1-2-6" and "1-5-6" are **against the suji (essentially unlikely combinations)**

---

### Overtake suji (when an outside lane wins)

**Lanes 3–6 attack from the outside and take the first turn.**

#### Lane 3 overtake
| Development | Suji combination | Reason |
|------|--------|------|
| Lane 3 overtake | 3-4, 3-5 | Outside boats follow the overtaking momentum |
| Lane 3 overtake-and-pass | 3-1, 3-4-1 | Lane 3 slips inside and takes the lead |

**Typical bets:**
```
3-4-5 (a pure overtake development)
3-4-1 (outside overtake + inside holds on)
3-1-4 (an inside-pass development)
```

#### Lane 4 overtake
| Development | Suji combination | Reason |
|------|--------|------|
| Lane 4 overtake | 4-5, 4-6 | Boats that swung wide together follow |
| Lane 4 overtake | 4-5-6 | A complete outside overtake sweep |

**Typical bets:**
```
4-5-6 (a big outside overtake)
4-5-1 (overtake + inside holds on)
4-3-5 (lane 3 holds for 2nd)
```

---

### Inside-pass suji

**The inside boat takes the lead at the turn, but gets passed on the inside.**

| Development | Suji combination | Reason |
|------|--------|------|
| Lane 2 inside pass | 2-1, 2-3 | Passes lane 1 on the inside |
| Lane 4 inside pass | 4-1, 4-2 | Looks like an overtake but passes on the inside |

**Key points:**
- An inside pass is a signature move of **racers skilled at turning**
- When an inside pass lands, **the original inside boat often still holds on for 2nd**

## Against the suji (unlikely combinations)

Understanding suji also tells you which combinations are **unlikely to come up**.

### Outside boats coming in despite an inside wire-to-wire (against the suji)
```
❌ 1-5-6 (rare for lanes 5 and 6 to finish 2nd/3rd behind a lane-1 wire-to-wire win)
❌ 1-6-5
❌ 1-6-2
```

### Inside boats coming in despite an outside overtake (against the suji)
```
❌ 4-1-2 (rare for lanes 1 and 2 to follow behind a lane-4 overtake win)
❌ 5-1-2
❌ 6-1-2
```

**When against-the-suji results happen:**
- A highly chaotic race
- A capsize or a racer falling overboard
- A wildly scattered start

Against-the-suji results **pay out big**, but they're hard to target deliberately.

## How to bet using suji

### Strategy 1: Narrow your bets with suji

**Use suji to trim down your bet combinations**

Example: a race where lane 1 looks likely to win
```
Every combination: 1-234-234 = 6 combinations
Narrowed by suji: 1-2-3, 1-2-4, 1-3-2, 1-3-4 = 4 combinations
```

Cutting just 2 combinations already improves your return rate.

---

### Strategy 2: Choose a suji from your predicted race development

**Watch the start exhibition, predict the development, and pick your suji accordingly**

```
[Predicted development]
- Lane 1's start looks slow
- Lane 4's start looks fast and likely to overtake
→ Go with the lane-4 overtake suji

[Bets]
4-5-6 (an overtake development)
4-5-1 (inside holds on)
4-3-5 (lane 3 holds for 2nd)
```

---

### Strategy 3: Split your bankroll between the main line and a hedge

**Put the suji combinations as your main line, and against-the-suji combinations as a hedge**

```
[Main line (suji combinations)] 80% of bankroll
1-2-3: ¥1,000
1-2-4: ¥800
1-3-2: ¥600

[Hedge (against the suji)] 20% of bankroll
1-5-4: ¥300
1-6-4: ¥300
```

## Suji tendencies by venue

Suji tendencies differ from venue to venue.

### Venues where an inside wire-to-wire suji is common
| Venue | Lane 1 win rate | Character |
|------|----------|------|
| Omura | ~60% | Japan's strongest inside advantage |
| Ashiya | ~58% | Calm water favors the inside |
| Tokuyama | ~57% | Calm water conditions |

→ **The 1-2-3 and 1-2-4 suji combinations land often**

### Venues where an overtake suji is common
| Venue | Character |
|------|------|
| Toda | Narrow water favors overtaking |
| Heiwajima | Tidal effects work against the inside |
| Edogawa | Difficult water conditions make things tough for the inside |

→ **The 3-4-5 and 4-5-6 suji combinations show up often**

## FAQ

### Q. Can I win just by betting the suji every time?

**A. Suji alone won't guarantee a win.**

Suji only represents "the pattern that tends to come up" — not a certainty. What matters just as much:
- Reading the race development from the start exhibition
- Racer ability and motor performance
- The effects of wind and waves

Think of suji as **a tool for narrowing down your bets**, not a complete answer on its own.

### Q. Should I avoid betting against the suji?

**A. It depends on the situation.**

- Normally, **prioritize the suji combinations**
- In a race that looks likely to be volatile, **hedge a small amount against the suji too**
- Against-the-suji payouts are large, so it's a reasonable form of insurance

### Q. How do I remember suji patterns?

**A. Remember it as "the boats near the winner tend to follow."**

- Lane 1 wins → lanes 2, 3 follow (nearby)
- Lane 4 wins → lanes 5, 6 follow (nearby)
- The exception is an inside pass, where an inside boat holds on

## Summary

### Key points on suji betting

1. **Suji is a theory of race development**
   - If an inside lane wins, inside lanes tend to follow
   - If an outside lane wins, outside lanes tend to follow

2. **Use suji to narrow your bets**
   - Betting every combination is capital-inefficient
   - Suji can trim your bets by 2–3 combinations

3. **Against-the-suji results are rare**
   - "1-5-6" and "4-1-2" are uncommon
   - They pay out big when they land, but they're hard to target

4. **Factor in venue character**
   - Inside-favoring venues → lean on inside wire-to-wire suji
   - Overtake-heavy venues → lean on overtake suji

5. **Combining it with AI works well**
   - AI prediction matching the suji → higher confidence
   - AI prediction going against the suji → proceed cautiously
