## What is return rate? First, understand "the 25% deduction wall"

Roughly **25% of every yen wagered on boat racing is taken as the operator's cut (deduction rate)**. The remaining 75% is returned to bettors.

| Item | Value |
|------|------|
| Return rate on total wagers | ~75% |
| Deduction rate | ~25% |
| Expected return rate on random betting | **~75%** |

In other words, betting randomly turns **¥10,000 into ¥7,500** as a mathematical inevitability. Breaking a 100% return rate means overcoming this 25% handicap through strategy.

---

## Why most people lose — 3 causes the data reveals

### 1. Betting on every race

Over 100 races run nationwide every day. Betting on all of them raises your total hit count, but **betting even on low-expected-value races** is the biggest reason bettors lose overall.

### 2. Changing your bet size based on emotion

"I just lost, so I'll double up to win it back." Repeat this pattern, and a single bad decision can wipe out an entire day's profit.

### 3. Spreading your combinations too thin

A 60- or 100-combination box bet on a Trifecta raises your hit rate, but leads to frequent **break-even hits (winning the bet but still losing money)**.

## 5 strategies to raise your return rate

### ⭐ Strategy 1: Choose your races (the most important one)

The single biggest secret to breaking a 100% return rate is **deciding which races NOT to bet on**.

**Race conditions worth targeting:**
- A Class A1 racer in lane 1 with a motor 2nd-place rate of 40%+
- A calm-water venue that favors the inside lane (Omura, Tokuyama, Ashiya, etc.)
- A race with a clear gap in racer ability

**Races to avoid:**
- A race where every racer's ability is closely matched
- Bad weather or strong-wind conditions
- SG/G1 finals (closely matched skill levels make upsets more likely)

### ⭐ Strategy 2: Use different bet types strategically

| Bet type | Approx. hit rate | Best suited for |
|---------|-----------|---------------|
| Place | 50–60% | Steadily building up profit on solid races |
| Quinella | 15–25% | Races with a clear anchor |
| Trio | 8–15% | A balanced approach targeting a mid-range payout |
| Trifecta | 3–8% | A big payout on races with a predictable development |

![Typical hit rate by bet type. Place 50-60%, Quinella 15-25%, Trio 8-15%, Trifecta 3-8% — the higher the hit rate, the easier it is to stabilize your return rate](/images/blog/improve-recovery-rate-hitrate-en.jpg)

For a stable return rate, focusing on **Place and Quinella, with Trio for the races you're most confident in**, works well.

### ⭐ Strategy 3: Narrow your combination count

**Rule of thumb: (combination count × ¥100) < the expected minimum payout**

- Cap Trio bets at **6–10 combinations**
- Cap Trifecta bets at **12–20 combinations**
- Beyond that, the risk of a break-even hit rises sharply

### ⭐ Strategy 4: Manage your bankroll strictly

**Recommended rules:**
- Set a daily spending cap (e.g., ¥5,000)
- Fix your bet size per race (e.g., ¥500)
- Never chase a loss with a bigger bet
- Keep any profit separate from your principal

Removing emotion and **running it mechanically** is what drives long-term improvement in return rate.

### ⭐ Strategy 5: Use AI and data

If it takes a human 5–10 minutes to analyze a single race's entry list, checking all 100+ races in a day is simply impossible.

AI can analyze every race in seconds and **extract only the statistically favorable ones**. Judgment based on data — not "gut feeling" or "experience" — is what pushes return rate up.

## Solid races vs. volatile races — using each one right drives your return rate

### Characteristics of a solid race (favorite wins)

- A pattern where lane 1's Class A1 racer wins wire-to-wire
- Low payouts (Trio around ¥500–1,500) but a high hit rate
- ✅ Best suited for **steadily building up profit with Place or Quinella bets**

### Characteristics of a volatile race (upset win)

- A close, tightly matched field
- Conditions with wind of 5m+ or high waves
- High payouts (a Trifecta can pay huge) but a low hit rate
- ✅ Best suited for **a small stake on Trio or Trifecta bets**

### Tips for using each

**Allocating 70% of your bankroll to solid races and 30% to volatile races** is a well-balanced strategy. Build steady gains on the solid races while chasing bigger returns on the volatile ones.

⚠️ Targeting only volatile races leads to long stretches between hits, which can wear down your discipline. Winning experiences on solid races form the foundation for staying calm and level-headed.
