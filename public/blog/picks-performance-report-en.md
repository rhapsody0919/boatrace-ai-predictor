## Why can it beat a 100% return rate?

### The difference between "predicting every race" and "curating"

BoatAI's standard predictions cover **every single race**. Naturally, some races are easier to predict than others. Averaged across everything, the return rate falls short of 100%.

"Today's Picks" takes a different approach:

**Thoroughly mine past data to find statistically proven conditions where the return rate exceeds 100%, then extract only the races that meet those conditions.**

In other words:
- Standard predictions → participate in every race and land on an average score
- Today's Picks → **only participate in the races you can win**

That "courage to pass" is the source of the over-100% return.

### The "distortions" data mining uncovers

Boat racing odds are shaped by the collective betting behavior of a huge number of fans. But human judgment has biases.

- Patterns where, under specific conditions at a specific venue, the odds run higher than the racer's actual ability warrants (i.e., undervalued by the market)
- Patterns where, for specific race numbers or time slots, a statistically favorable bet exists

These "odds distortions" only become visible through mechanical analysis of thousands of races — patterns that are hard to catch through human intuition or experience alone. **They're patterns only data can reveal.**

## What we should be honest about

### 104% isn't "a fortune"

Against a total wager of 2,577 races × ¥100 = ¥257,700, the profit is ¥11,470. Honestly, that's not enough to live on by itself.

But what matters is the fact that it **maintains a net-positive return**. Many prediction services are considered "excellent" at a 80–90% return rate — simply exceeding 100% is rare in itself.

### Not every rule wins consistently

"Today's Picks" is built from multiple patterns (rules). Among them:

- Rules that have performed well consistently since launch
- Rules currently going through a temporary rough patch

both exist side by side. The overall return rate holds at 104%, but **individual rules have their ups and downs**.

### Past performance doesn't guarantee the future

Even statistically valid patterns can shift over time due to changes in the boat racing environment — racer turnover, venue renovations, seasonal variation, and more.

BoatAI periodically re-validates each rule's effectiveness and revises any rule whose performance has deteriorated.
