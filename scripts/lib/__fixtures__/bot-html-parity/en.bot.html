Frequently asked questions

Can I see the AI boat race predictions for free?

Ryujin Radar's AI predictions are free, with no sign-up required. The past rates in Ryujin Sonar can also be checked without signing up.

What data can I check when looking at the AI predictions?

In Ryujin Sonar, you can check the results of past races under close conditions and the past rates for each entry or start pattern. The race card also shows racer and motor records.

In what order should I read Ryujin Sonar?

Check the date, venue, race number and race card first, then read the conditions, period and number of races in Ryujin Sonar.

Can I use it before the exhibition?

Before the exhibition, you can still check the race card records and past race tendencies. Exhibition times and other same-day values appear after the exhibition, so "preparing" or "—" until then does not mean zero.

Are the past rates the predicted probability for this race?

The past rates are the results of past races under the conditions you chose on screen, not the predicted probability for this race. Read them together with the conditions, period, number of races, and how finishing order and refunds are handled, and do not use them as a guaranteed number.
